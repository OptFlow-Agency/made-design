# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses of any kind in Morocco and internationally that need a professional online presence, or need to reshape the one they have. Broad by intent: from small businesses to growth-stage brands, not a single sector. They arrive after an ad, a referral or a post, deciding whether to hire a studio to design and build their website.

## Product Purpose

Optflow is a studio that designs and creates websites for businesses. The marketing site exists to win client inquiries; success is a visitor starting a conversation (closing CTA: "Let's talk about your project").

## Positioning

A design-led studio with a performance edge: premium craft first, with fast, search-ready sites as the substance. Honest about what a website does: it supports a business's sales effort and does not replace its team or strategy, and it guarantees no leads or sales.

## Operating Context

Single-page site, modelled on the structure and tone of the ImmoVue reference site (https://immovue-gdz7ghbi.manus.space/): hero; why a site (interest starts elsewhere, trust is built on your own site); six benefits; three starting-point formats (business site, landing page, multi-page brand site) with scope, content and budget defined after a conversation; a work gallery of real sites with preview links; process; contact. No pricing table, clients, testimonials, journal or FAQ at present.

The site is to be available in French and English (FR/EN), serving Morocco and international visitors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services described in copy: website design and build, search-ready structure, performance, brand identity.
- Formats are starting points, not fixed packages. Exact scope, packages and pricing are not confirmed.
- Existing stack: Astro 7 with framer-motion, lenis (smooth scroll), three.js and lucide-react; custom canvas scripts (grid-wave, pixel-cloud, particle-network).
- Current copy is English only; French is a confirmed requirement, not yet implemented.
- Contact details in `src/data/site.ts` (email, phone, address) are placeholders.

## Evidence on Hand

Four real work samples, supplied by the user as hero screenshots in `src/assets/work/`: Inkwell (landing page for a markdown note-taking app), Lumina Studio (interior design business site), Teressa (landing page for a UI-components platform), Thockcraft (mechanical-keyboard e-commerce site). Live URLs are still to be supplied. Their copy and any claims inside the screenshots belong to those sites, not to Optflow.

Still no clients' results, testimonials, case studies or benchmarks. Future work must not invent any, and must not show fabricated stats, logos or quotes; use clearly neutral placeholder content, or leave the slot out, until real material exists.

## Product Principles

1. Craft is the credential: the site itself must demonstrate the quality it sells.
2. Performance is part of the design: speed, crawlability and search-readiness are never traded away for effect.
3. Claims need proof: no result, client or number appears without real evidence behind it; promise clarity, not outcomes.
4. Speak to any business: clear for a small-business owner, credible to a marketing lead.
5. Bilingual from the start: FR and EN carry equal weight for Moroccan and international visitors.
